# YouTube Shorts Niche Performance 2025–2026: Views, Engagement, Growth Speed, Algorithm Fit, India

> **Method note for the report writer:** WebFetch was blocked in this session for every domain tried (ytgrowth.io, shortsintel.com, outlierkit.com, air.io all failed DNS resolution under the sandbox network policy). Every finding below comes from **search-result summaries of the cited pages**, not full-page reads. Treat exact numbers as "reported by source X," and re-check them before you publish. Almost all niche-level numbers come from **vendor or marketing blogs** (labelled [VENDOR/BLOG]). The only primary or official sources found are YouTube/Neal Mohan's statements (reported via press) and the YouTube India 2025 year-end list (reported via press). No 2025–2026 Shorts niche dataset was found from TubeBuddy, Socialinsider, Rival IQ, HubSpot, Hootsuite or Sprout Social. Geography is global unless marked otherwise.

## Q1. Which Shorts niches show the highest average views and engagement rates (2025–2026)?

### Takeaway
No authoritative, method-consistent 2025–2026 study ranks Shorts niches. The best available signals are two 2026 vendor studies, and they disagree. Both put **gaming** at or near the top for Shorts engagement. One ranks **education/how-to** first, and **cooking/food** comes out lowest on engagement in the ytgrowth study. The best views-per-Short data by category (Tubular Labs, **Food & Drink** and **Home/DIY** leading) dates from **H1 2023** and is outdated. Entertainment holds the largest *share* of Shorts views (TubeBuddy).

### Cited Findings
**Platform scale (official, global)**
- Neal Mohan (YouTube CEO) said at Cannes Lions in June 2025 that "YouTube Shorts are now averaging over 200 billion daily views." Coverage reports this as ~186% above the 70B daily views figure from March 2024. It is company-reported and not independently verified. A vidIQ host questioned the maths, which implies ~75 Shorts per user per day. Some outlets wrote "nearly" rather than "over." As of an April 2026 roundup, it was still the latest official milestone. — [Tubefilter, Jun 18 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/06/18/youtube-shorts-200-billion-daily-views-google-veo-3-ai-neal-mohan/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-reports-200-billion-daily-views-for-shorts-format/549315/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/youtube-shorts-200-billion-daily-views)

**Engagement rate by niche, Shorts (global, 2026, [VENDOR/BLOG])**
- **ytgrowth.io, 49,802-video study (2026):** **Gaming** Shorts have the highest median engagement rate of any niche at **3.613%**. **Music** is second at **2.785%** and **cooking** is lowest at **1.674%**. The formula is (likes + comments) / views. The study also finds that **long-form beat Shorts in every niche with enough data, by a median of 1.4×.** — [ytgrowth.io](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-engagement-rate)
- **SociaVault Labs, "75,000 channels" (2026):** niche medians are **Education & How-to 4.62%**, **Gaming 4.18%**, **Technology Reviews 3.94%** and **Fitness & Health 3.42%**. The search summaries do not make clear whether these are Shorts-only. SociaVault says **Shorts median ER is ~3.8% vs ~2.7% for long-form (1.4× higher)**, which directly contradicts ytgrowth. Its related pages describe a "350,000+ accounts across six platforms" dataset, so the sample definition is unclear. — [SociaVault](https://sociavault.com/blog/good-engagement-rate-youtube); [SociaVault education vs beauty](https://sociavault.com/blog/education-vs-beauty-niche-engagement-gap-2026)
- Finance/investing: the only figure found is **2.8% average ER (range 2.0–3.5%)**. It comes from a calculator site with no named source study, so it is unverified. — [flowshorts.app](https://flowshorts.app/tools/youtube-engagement-rate-calculator)
- Platform-wide Shorts ER is commonly cited at **~5.9%**, attributed to Statista, vs TikTok 5.75% and Reels 5.53%. Socialinsider's 2025 figure for **brand** accounts is far lower at **0.40%**. Formulas and samples differ widely between these sources. — [Thunderbit](https://thunderbit.com/blog/youtube-shorts-statistics-engagement-metrics); [adamconnell.me](https://adamconnell.me/youtube-shorts-statistics/); [posteverywhere.ai](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/social-media-engagement-rate-benchmarks)
- An unattributed blog stat ranks engagement by content type: **humor 48.2%, entertainment 39.1%, pets 27%, food 23.5%**. No source or method is given, and the percentages are not standard ER. Treat it as unreliable. — reported in search summaries of [adamconnell.me](https://adamconnell.me/youtube-shorts-statistics/) / [demandsage](https://www.demandsage.com/youtube-shorts-statistics/)

**Views by niche/category (global)**
- **Tubular Labs, H1 2023 (Jan 1–Jun 30 2023; outdated):** **Food & Drink** had the highest average views per Short at **~989K**, with **Home, DIY & Crafts** second at **~921K**. A later Tubular LinkedIn post (World Cup-era comparison) gives Food & Drink **~859K**. — [Tubular H2 2023 Social Video Trends PDF](https://lp.tubularlabs.com/hubfs/TUB-Guides%20Resources/H2_2023_Social_Video_Trends___Tubular.pdf); [adamconnell.me](https://adamconnell.me/youtube-shorts-statistics/)
- **TubeBuddy (30M videos; date unclear):** **Entertainment** Shorts made up **17% of all Shorts views**. A widely repeated "entertainment 32% / gaming 21% / how-to 18%" split could not be traced to a primary study. — [adamconnell.me](https://adamconnell.me/youtube-shorts-statistics/); [kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/best-niches-youtube-shorts-2026/)
- An **average Short gets ~647 views** (Metricool-based), vs ~18,173 for TikTok and ~16,153 for Reels. A different TubeStats figure of "22.5M avg views per Short" clearly uses an incompatible method. — [hookclone](https://www.hookclone.com/blog/how-many-views-on-youtube-shorts-is-good); [tubestats.io](https://tubestats.io/statistics/shorts)
- Channels **under 1K subs typically get 200–1,000 views per Short** [VENDOR/BLOG]. Business/Finance Shorts are estimated at **2,000–25,000 views per Short** [vendor estimate]. — [hookclone](https://www.hookclone.com/blog/how-many-views-on-youtube-shorts-is-good)
- Average watch time is **~14.3 s per Short**. Shorts with **50+ s average view duration reportedly averaged ~4.1M views**, attributed to Sprout Social via a blog and not verified. **50–60 s Shorts get the most views**, per Inflow Network via a blog. — [hookclone search summary](https://www.hookclone.com/blog/how-many-views-on-youtube-shorts-is-good); [adamconnell.me](https://adamconnell.me/youtube-shorts-statistics/)

**Monetization by niche (context for niche choice; global)**
- **AIR Media-Tech, 274 channels (2026):** Shorts RPM ranges **$0.02–$1.48** by niche. Other sources give $0.01–$0.07 (Miraflow) and $0.08–$0.35 for finance (Fluxnote). Personal finance and tech/AI are repeatedly cited as the highest-RPM Shorts niches (~$0.15–$0.25 per 1K, one source). The figure of "up to $4.50 CPM" for finance is advertiser CPM, not creator RPM. Shorts RPM is calculated per 1,000 **engaged** views. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026); [Miraflow](https://miraflow.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-2026-real-ranges-by-niche); [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-niche-selection-income-2026); [gyre.pro](https://gyre.pro/blog/the-most-profitable-youtube-shorts-niches-for-beginners)

### Inferences
- Gaming is the one niche both 2026 engagement studies place high on Shorts. Education/how-to and tech are high in SociaVault. Food/cooking draws big reach (Tubular 2023) but low per-view engagement (ytgrowth 2026). This fits a "high-reach, low-interaction, passive-scroll" profile.
- Engagement percentages from different vendors cannot be compared directly. They use different formulas (some divide by views, some by followers), different samples (brands vs creators), and the view-counting change of March 31 2025 enlarged the denominators.
- There is an inverse relationship between reach and RPM. Entertainment/comedy/food have the largest audiences, while finance/tech/AI pay the most per view and get fewer views per Short.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 views-per-Short-by-niche dataset was found from any named analytics firm (Tubular, Playboard, Social Blade, TubeBuddy, vidIQ). Tubular's category data is from 2023.
- No niche-specific data was found for: true crime/mystery, history/storytelling, motivation, ASMR/satisfying, pets/animals, cricket/sports, career/business or Bollywood. Only qualitative blog rankings exist for these.
- Likes vs comments vs shares were never broken out by niche in any source found.
- Socialinsider, Rival IQ, HubSpot, Hootsuite and Sprout Social Shorts niche benchmarks did not surface in searches.

## Q2. Which niches are rising (momentum) vs saturated or declining as of late 2026?

### Takeaway
Hard data on momentum is sparse. The clearest documented rising formats are **microdramas / episodic Shorts**, **AI tools & workflow content**, **non-verbal / visual-comedy Shorts** and **multi-language dubbed content** (India). The clearest documented pressure point is **mass-produced, templated faceless AI content**, which has been under monetization enforcement since July 2025. No dataset was found that quantifies which niches are *declining*.

### Cited Findings
- **Microdramas:** they passed **6.5 billion views on YouTube in H1 2026**, with **watch time up 50%+ YoY**. YouTube added **season/episode grouping for Shorts in September 2026**. (Social Blade-based roundup, [VENDOR/BLOG].) — [AIR Media-Tech fastest-growing niches 2026](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/whos-growing-fastest-on-youtube-and-which-niches-are-hot-in-2025)
- **AI tools / workflow content** is described by vidIQ as the **fastest-growing niche cluster of 2025–2026** (qualitative). — [vidIQ profitable AI niches](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/profitable-ai-niches-youtube/); [vidIQ best niches](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/best-youtube-niches/)
- **Saturation:** vidIQ says CPMs are lowest for **faceless AI channels in saturated formats (~$4–$12)** because many look-alike channels reach the same audience. vidIQ also says dense **B2B and finance** niches underperform on Shorts itself, although they can feed long-form. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/profitable-ai-niches-youtube/)
- **Policy headwind on templated/AI faceless content (global):** on **July 15 2025** YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to **"inauthentic content"** and clarified that mass-produced, templated, low-effort videos are ineligible for monetization. AI use itself is not banned. Enforcement is at channel level. Realistic synthetic content must be disclosed, and sensitive topics (health, news, elections, finance) need a visible AI label. — [Fliki](https://fliki.ai/blog/youtube-monetization-policy-2025); [AIR timeline](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-monetization-policy-changes-2026-a-complete-dated-timeline); [invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/youtube-kills-ai-faceless-channels/)
- In **December 2025**, YouTube reportedly **terminated Screen Culture (India) and KH Studio (USA)** for repeated AI-generated fake movie trailers. An IBTimes UK figure ("11 channels terminated, ~$10M/yr lost revenue") is reported second-hand and unverified. — [outlierkit AI crackdown](https://outlierkit.com/blog/youtube-ai-crackdown)
- **Rising, from YouTube's own trend reports (via press, Dec 2025):** **non-verbal / silent visual Shorts** (KL BRO Biju Rithvik in India; the Korean collective KIMPRO's nonverbal challenges) and **multi-language audio dubbing** (MrBeast gained ~47–50M subscribers from India in 2025). The global Culture & Trends report covered 12 markets, including India. — [netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-global-culture-and-trends-2025-platform-highlights-creator-innovation-and-regional-content-shifts/); [Tubefilter Dec 15 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/12/15/youtube-2025-culture-and-trends/)
- GRWM videos in India drew **7.2B+ views in 2024**, and skincare Shorts **4B+ views** (2024 data, India). — [netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-global-culture-and-trends-2025-platform-highlights-creator-innovation-and-regional-content-shifts/)

### Inferences
- Momentum seems to favour **formats** (episodic/serialized, non-verbal, dubbed) more than topic niches. Episodic history/storytelling, true-crime or "mystery" series could ride the microdrama / series-grouping tailwind. This is my inference and is not measured.
- Faceless AI niches that depend on templated output (generic facts, Reddit-story TTS, AI "fake trailers", motivation-quote slideshows) face both saturation and policy risk. AI-assisted content with original scripting or a creator voice is not restricted by policy.
- Finance and B2B are strong for RPM but weak for Shorts reach. They may work better as Shorts-to-long-form funnels.

### Gaps
- No quantitative "declining niche" data (e.g., year-over-year view-share loss by category) was found from vidIQ, Tubular or Playboard.
- "Brainrot" or meme-format trends (e.g., the 2025 Italian brainrot wave) were not researched or found with data.
- No late-2026 (Q3–Q4) niche trend report was found. The newest items are the September 2026 Shorts series grouping and H1 2026 microdrama stats.

## Q3. How fast do new Shorts channels reach 1K / 10K / 100K subscribers? Case studies (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
No rigorous dataset on time-to-milestone by niche exists. Documented experiments show that **typical** new Shorts channels grow slowly: Kapwing's faceless channel took ~6 months to reach 1K, and a 30-day test produced 12 subscribers. Fast-growth claims mostly come from course sellers. The only documented hyper-growth cases are mega-creators in India (Tech Master, Sirf Shreyansh) and one five-month-old history/storytelling channel at ~90K. Typical Shorts subscriber conversion is roughly **0.17–0.5% of views (≈1.7–5 subs per 1,000 views)**.

### Cited Findings
**Experiments / realistic baselines (global)**
- **Kapwing**'s own faceless channel experiment took **~6 months to reach 1,000 subscribers** despite consistent posting. — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/we-grew-a-faceless-youtube-channel-to-1000-subscribers/)
- A documented **30-day Shorts test** ended with **12 subscribers and 11,800 views over 24 days** (creator newsletter, anecdotal). — [Marina Mogilko Substack](https://marinamogilko.substack.com/p/how-long-it-takes-to-get-to-10k-subs)
- One creator took **787 days to reach 100K** and attributed later growth to titles/thumbnails over upload frequency (anecdotal, mixed format). — [verynormal Substack](https://verynormal.substack.com/p/issue-41-a-lifelong-goal-achieved)
- A frameco.app guide estimates faceless channels reach **0→100K in 8–9 months (best case)** and **12–14 months (realistic)**. These are the author's observations, not data [VENDOR/BLOG]. — [frameco.app](https://frameco.app/blog/how-to-grow-faceless-youtube-channel-0-to-100k-subscribers)
- Course/sales pages promise "10K→200K in 2 months" or "100K+ in under a month" with no evidence. Exclude these as marketing. — [gumroad example](https://successfulcreeds.gumroad.com/l/youtubemasstery)

**Fast-growth case studies**
- **History/storytelling (faceless):** a channel making "Secret Lives of Ancient Humans" videos reached **~90K subscribers at five months old** (2026 listing, global). — [taletok.io](https://taletok.io/blog/best-faceless-youtube-channels/)
- **Tech Master (India, tech/gadget Shorts):** **~16.3M subs (Jan 2025) → 20M (Apr 2025) → ~36.1M (Nov 1 2026)**, with **~23.45B total views**. In mid-2025 it was gaining ~0.6–1.2M subscribers a month (vidIQ tracker). "Tech Master Shorts" is named on the YouTube India 2025 top creators list. It is unclear whether that name refers to the same channel or a Shorts sub-channel. — [vidIQ Tech Master](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@techmasterhq/); [trendreport](https://trendreport.com/channels/stats/techmasterhq/); [Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/technology/tech-news/youtube-india-releases-its-2025-year-end-trend-list-1030852)
- **Sirf Shreyansh (India, Shorts-led):** **~8.23M subs (Apr 2025) → 11.1M (Aug 2025, Social Blade) → ~16.2M (Nov 1 2026)**, with ~15.2B views from 659 videos. It gained ~0.5–1M subscribers a month in late 2025. It is on the YouTube India 2025 top creators list. — [vidIQ Sirf Shreyansh](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@sirfshreyansh/); [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/sirfshreyansh)
- **KL BRO Biju Rithvik (India, silent family comedy):** **~79M subscribers**, highlighted by YouTube India 2025 for silent comedy. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/12/15/youtube-2025-culture-and-trends/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KL_Bro_Biju)
- These channels launched **before 2024**. They show growth *rates* for established creators, not new-channel launch speed.

**Subscriber conversion benchmarks (global, [VENDOR/BLOG])**
- The most-cited figure is **16.9 subscribers per 10,000 Shorts views** (≈0.17%, i.e., ~1.7 per 1,000). It is attributed to Adam Connell's 2025 work and echoed by Stripo and Ylytic, probably from one origin. One page wrongly converts it to 1.69%. — [Stripo research](https://research.stripo.email/youtube-benchmarks); [Ylytic](https://www.ylytic.com/blog/blog-youtube-benchmarks-2026)
- Other ranges are **0.2–1.0% typical**, **0.3–0.8% of unique viewers** (attributed to vidIQ / Creator Insider 2025, unverified), and a target of **>0.5%**, with **>1% strong**. — [retensis](https://retensis.com/blog/youtube-shorts-statistics-2026); [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-to-subscribers-strategy-2026); [owlclaw](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/youtube-shorts-benchmarks/)
- A comparison claims TikTok leads on follower conversion, while Shorts slightly leads on engagement [VENDOR/BLOG]. — [Thunderbit](https://thunderbit.com/blog/youtube-shorts-statistics-engagement-metrics)
- Monetization threshold for the Shorts route: **1,000 subscribers + 10M valid Shorts views in 90 days** (or 4,000 watch hours via long-form). — [virvid.ai](https://virvid.ai/blog/ai-faceless-youtube-channel-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- Rough sizing: at ~0.17–0.5% conversion, **1K subs needs ~200K–600K Shorts views**, **10K needs ~2–6M** and **100K needs ~20–60M**. This is derived arithmetic, not observed data. Niches with higher conversion (education, tech, series/storytelling where viewers want "part 2") may get there faster than passive-scroll niches (satisfying/ASMR, food), but no study measured this.
- Story/series formats (history, episodic) show the clearest documented case of fast growth for a new channel (~90K in five months).

### Gaps
- No dataset on time-to-1K/10K/100K **by niche** was found.
- No verified case studies were found of channels **launched 2024–2026** in finance, motivation, cricket, Bollywood or true crime with documented timelines.
- Reddit r/NewTubers threads did not surface in search, so community-reported timelines are missing.

## Q4. What does the 2025–2026 Shorts algorithm reward, and which niches fit?

### Takeaway
YouTube says the system optimizes for viewer **satisfaction**, using surveys alongside engagement, and that recommendations are mostly "pulled" per viewer. Third-party sources consistently describe a Shorts flow in which a seed audience is tested first, and the **viewed-vs-swiped-away** ratio, retention/loops, replays and shares then decide whether distribution widens. Since **March 31 2025**, a Short's "view" counts as soon as it plays or replays. "Engaged views" (the old stricter count) remain the basis for monetization.

### Cited Findings
- **Satisfaction (YouTube, Todd Beaupré, Creator Insider):** the algorithm looks at satisfaction, collected through surveys as well as engagement. Adding feedback signals makes people come back to YouTube more over time. In a **September 2026** Creator Insider interview, Beaupré said surveys separate time spent from satisfaction and that "most of the recommendations are more pulled for each viewer." It was also reported that **subscribers skip ~90% of uploads in their feed**. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-algorithm/); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/)
- **View counting change:** from **March 31 2025**, a Shorts view counts as soon as playback starts or restarts, with no minimum watch time. The old definition lives on as **"engaged views"** in Analytics, which YPP eligibility and Shorts revenue use. An unsourced claim says raw views jumped 20–30% after the change. — [shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-does-youtube-shorts-algorithm-work); [growcreator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/viewed-vs-swiped-away-youtube-shorts); [ytgrowth.io](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-engagement-rate)
- **Viewed vs swiped away** measures the share of feed impressions where people chose to watch instead of scrolling past. It is described as the key early test-audience signal [third-party]. — [growcreator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/viewed-vs-swiped-away-youtube-shorts); [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/)
- **Benchmarks (community / vendor; YouTube publishes none):** **≥70% viewed** is workable and **≥80% viewed** is strong. Other guides say swipe-away should be **10–30%** or **<20%**. A claim attributed to Tubular's 2025 report says swipe-away under 30% drove ~4× the sustained distribution of swipe-away over 50%; this is unverified. Several guides say to benchmark against your own 30–90-day median, not a niche norm. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/); [humbleandbrag](https://humbleandbrag.com/blog/youtube-shorts-benchmarks); [retensis](https://retensis.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-explained)
- **vidIQ (citing Shorts product lead Todd Sherman):** Shorts lean on the swipe-vs-watch ratio in the first 1–3 s, replay rate and shares. A Short is tested on a small seed audience, and distribution stops if engagement is weak (attribution of specific weights unverified). — [vidIQ Shorts algorithm](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [vidIQ algorithm](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/understanding-youtube-algorithm/)
- Reports mention a "December 2025 algorithm shift" without detail. Some blogs date the view-count change to "March 2024", which conflicts with March 31 2025. — [gyre.pro](https://gyre.pro/blog/understanding-the-youtube-shorts-algorithm-a--guide)

### Inferences (niche fit)
- **Strong fit for swipe-stopping plus loops:** satisfying/ASMR, pets/animals, comedy/skits, food (visual hooks in the first second, short and loopable). Food/DIY had the highest Tubular views per Short (2023).
- **Strong fit for shares and comments:** facts/curiosity, AI/tech explainers, finance tips ("send to a friend", debate-worthy). Education and tech rank high on SociaVault engagement.
- **Strong fit for satisfaction plus return visits:** serialized storytelling (history, true crime, microdramas). The September 2026 series grouping supports the "watch next part" behaviour. Microdrama watch time grew 50%+ YoY.
- **Language-agnostic fit:** non-verbal comedy and visual formats travel across India's language markets (KL BRO Biju, KIMPRO).
- These fits are reasoned from signal mechanics. No source measured algorithm fit by niche.

### Gaps
- No primary YouTube documentation was retrieved on Shorts-specific ranking signal weights. Only secondary summaries exist.
- No niche-level benchmarks for viewed-vs-swiped, average % viewed or loop rate were found.
