# YouTube Shorts Monetization by Niche & Geography (2025–2026), India-based Creator Focus

> Research note (compiled 2026-11-03). **Method caveat:** In this session, direct page fetches were blocked for support.google.com, air.io, searchenginejournal.com and business-standard.com. Everything below comes from search-result snippets and summaries. YouTube Help Center content appears only as snippets. Most RPM, brand-deal and affiliate figures come from third-party SEO, agency or tool-vendor blogs. YouTube publishes no official Shorts RPM by niche or country. Treat every number as an estimate unless it is marked as official.
> Currency assumption used in the Inferences sections only: ~₹88 = $1 (approximate 2025–26 rate; not sourced).

## 1. YPP thresholds (current 2026 rules, the announced Feb 2027 change, and India applicability)

### Takeaway
Through 31 Jan 2027 the two-tier structure the brief describes still holds. Fan funding needs 500 subscribers + 3 uploads in 90 days + 3M Shorts views in 90 days (or 3,000 watch hours in 12 months). Ad revenue share needs 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days (or 4,000 watch hours). India is an eligible country for both. YouTube reportedly announced changes on ~10 Aug 2026, effective **1 Feb 2027**. New full-YPP applicants would need **20M Shorts views in 90 days or 8,000 watch hours**. Every channel would also need a **rolling 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days** to keep earning from the Shorts Creator Pool. This changes the "path to first income" for a new India Shorts channel.

### Cited Findings
**Current (2026) rules**
- Fan-funding tier: 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 3M public Shorts views in 90 days. Full ad-revenue tier: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. — [vidIQ YPP guide](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-guide/); [Brolly Academy](https://brollyacademy.com/youtube-monetization-rules/); [AIR Media-Tech YPP 2026 guide](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-partner-program-requirements-2026-the-complete-guide)
- India is listed among the countries where YPP is available. — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/apps/youtube-partner-program-eligibility-requirements) (citing YouTube Help's availability list); [StatFlare (India)](https://www.statflare.in/blog/youtube-partner-program-requirements-2026)
- Shorts-feed views do not count toward the 4,000 long-form watch hours; the two are separate paths. Views from private, unlisted or deleted Shorts, from ad campaigns, and from Image Posts don't count toward the 3M/10M Shorts total. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-partner-program/); [Nexlev](https://www.nexlev.io/youtube-partner-program-requirements)
- The 500-sub / 3M tier unlocks fan funding only (Memberships, Super Thanks/Chat/Stickers, and Shopping per some guides). It does **not** unlock Shorts ad revenue sharing. — [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-monetization-requirements-2026-explained); [vidIQ Shorts monetization](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/)

**Announced change, effective 1 Feb 2027 (secondary coverage; official Help page seen only as a snippet)**
- YouTube Help has a page titled "Changes to the YouTube Partner Program". Existing partners must accept updated terms in YouTube Studio by 31 Jan 2027. If they don't, they stop earning from the associated monetization features from 1 Feb 2027. Accepting does not change YPP status. — [YouTube Help: Changes to YPP (snippet)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en)
- New applicants: 1,000 subs (unchanged) + 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days OR 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days (up from 4,000 / 10M). — [Business Standard, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/technology-tech-news-youtube-partner-program-monetisation-rules-2027-watch-hours-shorts-views-126081100708_1.html); [vidIQ 2027 changes](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/); [SocialDay](https://socialday.live/features/youtube-raises-partner-program-entry-bar-to-8-000-watch-hours-from-february-2027)
- Ongoing Shorts payout floor: a channel below 10M qualified Shorts views in the trailing 90 days stops earning from the Shorts Creator Pool for that period. It stays in YPP, keeps long-form earnings, and resumes Shorts revenue automatically once it is back above 10M. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-updates-partner-program-terms-shorts-payout-rules/585331/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/); [HowSociable](https://howsociable.com/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization-requirements)
- A new "active channel" requirement: at least 1,000 qualified watch hours in the past year, OR 1M qualified Shorts views in 90 days, OR 2 long-form uploads / 5 Shorts every 90 days. — [Contentmonetizing.com](https://contentmonetizing.com/en/youtube-partner-program-2027/); [CineD](https://www.cined.com/youtube-makes-monetization-harder-new-partner-program-rules-explained/)
- YouTube says total creator investment is unchanged and expects to pay out more in 2027 than in 2026. — [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/youtube-partner-program-changes); [CineD](https://www.cined.com/youtube-makes-monetization-harder-new-partner-program-rules-explained/)
- **Conflict:** Analytics Insight says existing YPP creators won't face the new entry threshold. That is consistent with "20M applies to new applicants only", but it seems to clash with the 10M rolling floor, which other outlets say applies to all channels. — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/apps/youtube-partner-program-eligibility-requirements) vs [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-updates-partner-program-terms-shorts-payout-rules/585331/)
- No source found says the 500-sub / 3M fan-funding tier changes in 2027 (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- A creator starting now (Nov 2026) has about 3 months to hit 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views and apply under the old bar. After 1 Feb 2027, a Shorts-only applicant needs 20M views in 90 days (~222k views/day). Even once accepted, Shorts ad revenue stops in any 90-day window below 10M (~111k views/day).
- For a small Shorts-only channel, the 500-sub / 3M tier (fan funding plus, in India, Shopping affiliate; see §4) is now the most realistic first-income gate. Shorts AdSense becomes a "scale-only" revenue line.
- India applies the same global thresholds. No India-specific exception was found.

### Gaps
- The official YouTube Help/Blog text could not be fetched. The exact 2027 wording on who the 10M floor covers, and on qualified versus engaged views, is unverified.
- Whether the fan-funding tier (500 / 3M) changes in 2027: not found.

## 2. How Shorts ad revenue sharing works, and Shorts RPM ranges (global vs India vs US/UK)

### Takeaway
Shorts-feed ad revenue is pooled per country. A music-licensing cut comes off first, the remainder is split by each creator's share of engaged views in that country, and the creator keeps 45%. Typical Shorts RPM is **~$0.02–0.20 globally**. India-heavy audiences sit around **$0.008–0.04 (≈₹5–35 per 1,000 views)**. US audiences sit around **$0.05–0.33**, and US/UK finance or tech can reach $0.15–0.45 in a few reports. So US Shorts views pay roughly 10–40× India views.

### Cited Findings
**Mechanics**
- Ad revenue from ads between Shorts in the feed goes into a Creator Pool for each country. Music licensing is deducted first, by track count: a Short with 0 tracks sends 100% to the pool, 1 track sends 50%, 2 tracks send 33%. Each monetizing creator is allocated pool revenue in proportion to their share of engaged views from monetizing creators in that country. The creator keeps 45% of that allocation. — [AIR Media-Tech glossary](https://air.io/en/youtube-glossary/what-is-youtube-shorts-monetization-shorts-fund-vs-ad-revenue-share); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-monetization/); [ainfluencer](https://ainfluencer.com/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- Worked example (secondary): 100M engaged views in a country, $100,000 feed revenue, and 20% of Shorts using one track. Music deduction $10,000, pool $90,000. A creator with 1M engaged views (1%) is allocated $900 and receives $405. — [ainfluencer](https://ainfluencer.com/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- Only engaged views count (the viewer stays past the initial seconds; loops excluded). YouTube doesn't publish the exact threshold. — [ainfluencer](https://ainfluencer.com/youtube-shorts-monetization/); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- Program origin: revenue sharing for Shorts launched 1 Feb 2023 (older background). — [StreamTV Insider](https://www.streamtvinsider.com/video/youtube-shorts-monetization-start-february-1)

**RPM: global**
- AIR Media-Tech, 274 channels: Shorts RPM ranged from $0.02 to $1.48 per 1,000 views across niches. Most niches fall at ~$0.07–0.20. Shorts RPM is ~3–14% of long-form RPM in most niches, so ~11,000–34,000 Shorts views earn what 1,000 long-form views do. Music is the exception, with Shorts at 41–60% of its long-form RPM. — [AIR Media-Tech: Shorts vs long-form, 274 channels](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026); [Channel.farm summary](https://channel.farm/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-long-form-revenue-rpm-data)
- Common general benchmarks: $0.04–0.15 per 1,000 views; $0.02–0.12. — [vidIQ Shorts monetization](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/); [GrowCreator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-2026); [CreatorBlade](https://creatorblade.com/blog/shorts-monetization-strategy-2026)

**RPM: India vs US**
- US ~$0.328 vs India ~$0.008 per 1,000 Shorts views, about 41× (attributed to AIR Media-Tech's 2025 analysis). — [Fluxnote India vs US](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-revenue-india-vs-us-2026)
- India Shorts RPM $0.01–0.04. — [Fluxnote by country](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-rpm-by-country)
- India Shorts ₹5–30 per 1,000 views (vs ₹25–150 long-form). — [upGrowth, Sep 2026](https://upgrowth.in/youtube-cpm-india-guide-2026/); similar range in [Brolly Academy](https://brollyacademy.com/youtube-income-per-1000-views/)
- India Shorts ₹15–35 RPM; mass entertainment ₹15–45; premium niches such as personal finance ₹80–250. StoryK sells a tool, and its numbers run higher than others. — [StoryK RPM](https://storyk.in/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-india-2026-realistic-earnings-per-1000-views); [StoryK niches](https://storyk.in/blog/high-rpm-shorts-niches-indian-creators-2026)
- Per 1M Shorts views in India: ₹900–3,800 ([NewMoneyMatrix](https://newmoneymatrix.org/youtube-shorts-rpm-india/)) vs ₹3,000–12,000 ([Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/youtube-1-million-views-income-india-long-vs-shorts/)). **Sources disagree by roughly 10×.**
- US-audience Shorts: ~$0.03–0.08 per 1,000 views (vs $1–15 long-form) per one source. — [ytcalculators](https://ytcalculators.com/blog/youtube-rpm-by-niche/). This conflicts with Fluxnote/AIR's $0.328 US figure.
- India long-form CPM is around $0.53, for context. — [ytcalculators CPM by country](https://ytcalculators.com/blog/youtube-cpm-by-country/)

### Inferences
- Revenue on exactly the 10M-view qualifying minimum (90 days), using the ranges above:
  - India-heavy audience at $0.008–0.04: **$80–400 (≈₹7,000–35,000) per 90 days**.
  - US-heavy audience at $0.05–0.33: **$500–3,300 (≈₹44,000–2.9 lakh) per 90 days**.
- Using no copyrighted music keeps 100% of a Short's revenue in the pool, versus 50% with one track. For a pure-AdSense Shorts strategy, original or no-music audio is a direct ~2× lever on that Short's attributable share.
- The pool is per country, so geography matters more than niche for Shorts AdSense. An India-audience finance Short probably earns less than a US-audience general Short.

### Gaps
- No primary YouTube data on Shorts RPM by country. The "41×" US/India figure is second-hand (Fluxnote citing AIR), and the AIR page could not be fetched to verify it.
- No reliable UK-specific Shorts RPM figure was found. Sources group "US/UK" together.
- No verified Reddit or creator screenshot data for 2025–26 surfaced in search.

## 3. Niche RPM: highest vs lowest, and Hindi vs English

### Takeaway
For Shorts, niche spreads are much narrower than for long-form. Most niches fall at ~$0.07–0.20. Finance, business, tech/AI and education sit at the top, roughly $0.04–0.45 (US-heavy). Kids, memes, comedy and gaming sit at the bottom, though no Shorts-specific ranking confirms the exact order. English-language content in India reportedly earns **2–5× the CPM** of pure Hindi content in the same niche.

### Cited Findings
- Finance/business Shorts: $0.03–0.17 ([Fluxnote finance](https://fluxnote.io/guides/finance-youtube-shorts-cpm-rates)); US/UK finance $0.04–0.21 ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/youtube-rpm-finance-niche)); finance/investing $0.15–0.45 ([CreatorBlade](https://creatorblade.com/blog/shorts-monetization-strategy-2026)).
- Tech/software/B2B/AI tools Shorts: ~$0.04–0.08 ([Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/most-profitable-ai-youtube-shorts-niches-2026-rpm-data)); tech reviews Q1 2026 median $0.31, range $0.12–0.68 ([GrowCreator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-2026)). **Sources conflict by roughly 4–8×.**
- Long-form niche ranking (AIR, 300 channels): Education & Science had the highest median RPM at $10.22; Kids & Teens the lowest at $0.33, ~30× less. — [AIR RPM by niche](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/which-youtube-niche-makes-the-most-money-in-2026-ranked-by-real-rpm-and-cpm); [Overseeros summary](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-rpm-by-niche). Gaming long-form ~$2.05 appears only on a third-party site (unverified).
- Music is the one niche where Shorts RPM approaches long-form (41–60%). — [AIR Shorts vs long-form](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- India finance long-form RPM is $0.50–1.60, vs $15–26 for US finance. India is 8–20× below the US for the same niche. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/youtube-rpm-finance-niche); [ytcalculators](https://ytcalculators.com/blog/youtube-rpm-by-niche/)
- **Hindi vs English:** English-language content in India earns ₹80–250 CPM vs ₹25–80 for pure Hindi content, 3–5× ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/youtube-cpm-in-india-complete-guide/)), or 2–5× ([IdentityKit](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/youtube-rpm-india-niche-2026)). The stated reasons: English reaches international ad inventory and premium Indian advertisers. Hindi entertainment/vlog channels earn ~$0.30–0.80 RPM (long-form context). — [Learning Revolution](https://www.learningrevolution.net/youtube-earnings-calculator/)
- Bilingual format (English titles, Hindi explanation) is described as a "sweet spot". Hindi is described as a "monster audience with low creator density"; one kids channel's Hindi dubs (30M+ monthly views) outdraw its English originals. — [IdentityKit](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/youtube-rpm-india-niche-2026); [AIR: languages to localize 2025](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/top-languages-to-localize-your-youtube-content-in-2025)

### Inferences
- Because the Shorts pool is split by views within each country rather than by advertiser value of the niche, niche probably shifts Shorts RPM less than geography does. This fits AIR's narrow band of ~$0.07–0.20 for most niches. Choose a high-RPM niche mainly for its off-platform value (brand deals, affiliate, products), not for the Shorts AdSense itself.
- Kids content is likely the worst Shorts choice for an India creator. It is the lowest long-form RPM niche, and Made-for-Kids channels are excluded from YouTube Shopping affiliate (see §4).

### Gaps
- No Shorts-only RPM ranking that names comedy, memes or gaming as lowest was found.
- No Hindi-vs-English data specific to Shorts was found. All language comparisons are for long-form CPM.

## 4. Non-AdSense revenue: brand deals, affiliate, Shopping, digital products (India)

### Takeaway
In India, brand deals are the dominant monetization route. Nano creators (1K–10K followers) earn ~₹1,000–15,000 per post. Micro creators (10K–100K) earn ~₹8,000–80,000, with YouTube integrations at up to ₹20,000–1 lakh+. Finance and tech creators command 30–60% higher rates. YouTube Shopping affiliate is live in India: it needs YPP (500-sub tier), and the merchants are Flipkart and Myntra, with Amazon tagging reportedly added. Amazon Associates India pays ~1–10% by category. One brand deal can out-earn months of India-audience Shorts AdSense.

### Cited Findings
**Brand deals (India; vendor rate cards, not audited data)**
- Nano (1K–10K): ₹2,000–12,000 per Reel ([upGrowth rate card](https://upgrowth.in/influencer-marketing-pricing-india-2026/)); ₹1,000–8,000 ([Grynow](https://www.grynow.in/blog/influencer-marketing-cost-in-india.html)).
- Micro (10K–100K): ~₹8,000–80,000, described as "the sweet spot for most Indian D2C brands". — [RuleScale](https://www.rulescale.in/blog/influencer-marketing-cost-india-2026); [TheShizz](https://theshizz.in/blog/influencer-marketing-rates-india)
- YouTube micro (10K–100K): ₹20,000–1,00,000+ for YouTube videos, depending on audience and depth (2025 guide). Shorts-specific pricing not found. — [Marketing Monster](https://marketingmonster.in/how-much-do-influencers-charge-in-india/)
- Macro: ₹2–12 lakh ([Jigsawkraft](https://www.jigsawkraft.com/post/influencer-marketing-cost-in-india-complete-2026-pricing-guide)); macro (500K–1M) ~₹3–15 lakh per post ([IdentityKit rate calculator](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/influencer-rate-calculator-india)).
- Finance and technology creators in India charge 30–60% more than lifestyle creators at the same follower count. — [IdentityKit rate calculator](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/influencer-rate-calculator-india)
- India's influencer market has "zero pricing transparency". — [TheShizz](https://theshizz.in/blog/influencer-marketing-rates-india)

**India creator-economy context**
- BCG (2025, "From Content to Commerce"): 2–2.5M active creators in India (>1,000 followers), with only 8–10% monetizing effectively vs 40%+ in mature markets. Direct creator-economy revenue is $20–25B, and creators influence $350B+ of consumer spend. — [BW Marketing World on BCG](https://www.bwmarketingworld.com/article/indias-creator-economy-to-drive-1-tn-by-2030-bcg-555691); [Kotak MF](https://www.kotakmf.com/Information/blogs/inside-india-creator-economy)

These measure different things.
- Brand deals are declining from ~80% to ~50% of income as affiliate and other streams grow (blog claim). — [Exif Media](https://www.exifmedia.com/blog/creator-economy-india-2025-trends)
- Kofluence puts India's active creators at 4.0–4.4M, vs BCG's 2–2.5M. The denominators differ. — [InfluencerMetric](https://influencermetric.in/blog/creator-economy-india-report)

**YouTube Shopping affiliate (India)**
- Eligibility: in YPP, meets the subscriber threshold, based in an eligible country (India listed), audience not Made for Kids, not a music channel. — [YouTube Help 13376398 (snippet)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)

Paid via AdSense 60–120 days after purchase; returns are reversed. — [Kudozz](https://www.kudozz.in/blog/youtube-shopping-india-creators); [Monetizedeal](https://blog.monetizedeal.com/newly-launched-youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-for-indian-creators/)

### Inferences
- **Path to first income for an India Shorts channel:** (1) affiliate links (Amazon Associates / EarnKaro need no YPP, so this works from day one); (2) at 500 subs + 3M Shorts views in 90 days, fan funding plus YouTube Shopping (Flipkart/Myntra/Amazon tags) in Shorts; (3) inbound brand deals at ~10K followers (₹5k–30k per integration); (4) Shorts AdSense only at 10M views in 90 days, rising to 20M for applicants from Feb 2027.
- Example: one micro brand deal (₹20,000) roughly equals the AdSense from ~5.5–25M India-audience Shorts views at ₹0.8–3.6 per 1,000. So for an India channel, brand-friendly niches (tech gadgets, personal finance, beauty/grooming, education/careers) matter more than AdSense RPM.
- Product-led niches (tech gadgets, fashion/beauty) suit Shopping/Amazon affiliate. Finance suits SaaS/fintech referrals and digital products. Memes and comedy monetize mainly through brand deals at scale.

## 5. Strategic trade-off: Hindi mass audience vs English/global audience

### Takeaway
Hindi or India-audience Shorts reach a huge audience but pay ~₹5–35 per 1,000 views. Those views mostly help a channel clear the view-count thresholds, which in turn unlock brand and affiliate income. English or global (US/UK) Shorts may pay ~10–40× more per view, but views are harder to win and Indian brand-deal access is weaker. The bilingual model (Hinglish or Hindi delivery, English titles, a high-intent niche) is the commonly recommended middle path.

### Cited Findings
- US ~41× India per Shorts view (~$0.328 vs ~$0.008). — [Fluxnote India vs US](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-revenue-india-vs-us-2026)
- English-language content earns 2–5× (or 3–5×) Hindi CPM in India in the same niche. — [IdentityKit](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/youtube-rpm-india-niche-2026); [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/youtube-cpm-in-india-complete-guide/)
- Hindi is a "monster audience with low creator density". The bilingual format (English titles, Hindi explanation) is a "sweet spot". — [IdentityKit](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/youtube-rpm-india-niche-2026); [AIR localization](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/top-languages-to-localize-your-youtube-content-in-2025)
- A US-heavy finance Short can out-earn the same view count spread across low-CPM regions by a wide margin. — [Fluxnote finance](https://fluxnote.io/guides/finance-youtube-shorts-cpm-rates)
- Finance and tech creators in India get 30–60% higher brand rates. — [IdentityKit rate calculator](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/influencer-rate-calculator-india)

### Inferences
- The 2027 rules (20M views to join, 10M rolling to keep Shorts pay) favour Hindi mass reach for hitting the volume thresholds. Revenue per view still favours English/global. The two strategies optimize different things.
- Illustrative 90-day comparison (assumes the ranges above, not sourced as a pair):
  - Hindi channel, 30M views at ₹0.8–3.5 per 1,000: ≈₹24,000–1 lakh AdSense, plus a larger Indian brand-deal pipeline.
  - English/global channel, 10M views at $0.05–0.33: ≈$500–3,300 (₹44,000–2.9 lakh), with a riskier view supply.
- For an India-based solo creator, the lower-risk play is probably Hindi/Hinglish delivery in a high-intent niche (personal finance, tech/AI tools, careers/education), monetized first through affiliate and brand deals. Shorts AdSense is a bonus at scale, and English/global is a secondary channel.

### Gaps
