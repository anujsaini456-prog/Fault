## Q1. Which sub-niches have the highest average views/engagement on Shorts, and which are trending up in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
I found no source that compares Shorts views or engagement across craft sub-niches (mehndi vs. crochet vs. resin, etc.). What exists is per-channel data. It shows that broad "general DIY/craft" channels in India reach very large scale. Crafter Aditi has about 15M subscribers and about 1M average Shorts views, but monthly growth has slowed to 0.41%. Mid-size craft channels average about 16K views per Short. The clearest platform-level signal is YouTube's own 2025 report: non-verbal Shorts helped Indian creators travel across languages. That favours hands-and-process craft content.

### Cited Findings
- **Crafter Aditi (@crafteraditi, India, general crafts/DIY):** about 15,055,987 subscribers (Oct 2026). Subscriber growth in Oct 2026 was 0.41%/month, which HypeAuditor rates "could be improved" against similar accounts. Average Shorts views about 993.6K. — [HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/youtube/UCr8CGgjyJpgdLVy8Duu2IrA/)
  - Other snapshots: 14.9M (Jul 2026) — [Qoruz](https://qoruz.com/ayushiaditicardzone/youtube); 73.1 lakh (about 7.3M) in a listicle that is about 3.5 years old (about 2022–23) — [Vavo Digital](https://vavodigital.com/diy-indian-youtubers/). That works out to roughly 2x subscriber growth over about 3–4 years. She also has a separate "Aditi Sharma Shorts" channel at about 990K subscribers (vidIQ figure, quoted in search summary) — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCPCPWj_qJKNTv-eb04tAAIg/). Note: her channel is **not faceless**. The creator appears on camera and is "famous for her cute voice, smile" — [Vavo Digital](https://vavodigital.com/diy-indian-youtubers/)
- **DIY Crafts TV (@diycraftstv):** about 2.18M subscribers. Engagement rate 4.73% ("Good" vs. similar). Average video views 17.3K; average **Shorts views 16.3K**. — [HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/youtube/diy_crafts_tv-UCv7tyVGWpJMiPIQXBHQ308Q/)
  - Inference: a 2M+ subscriber craft channel averaging only about 16K views per Short shows that subscriber count does not predict Shorts views in generic DIY.
- **Engagement rates, DIY & Home list:** Crafter Aditi 3.12%; Tonni art and craft 1.21%; 5-Minute Crafts SHORTS (14.8M subscribers) 1.26%. — [Influesque](https://influesque.com/top-youtube-channels/diy_home)
- **Engagement rates, DIY list (page about 530 days old, so about mid-2025):** DEV Ke Experiment 20M subscribers / 1.48% ER; Tonni art and craft 18M / 1.67%; Troom Troom India 13M. — [Influencers.club](https://influencers.club/top-youtubers/diy/). Different sites calculate engagement differently, so these rates are not directly comparable.
- **Other Indian craft channels:** Being Artistic 1.64M subscribers, about 301.92M views (2025 list) — [Beacons.ai](https://home.beacons.ai/blog/indian-diy-youtubers). Crafting with Rachna about 260K; Indian Crafts about 137K (Sept 2024 snapshot) — vidIQ via search summary ([vidIQ Indian Crafts](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCDy84YJGgzpaW-7pvbcY6vA), [vidIQ Crafting with Rachna](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCKHTtwfAhewEeAxgoS58jVg)).
- **Platform trend (official YouTube):** YouTube's 2025 Global Culture & Trends report says Indian creator success in 2025 "increasingly transcended regional and language boundaries through dubbed content, **non-verbal Shorts**, and internet-first phenomena." — [Net Influencer summarising YouTube Culture & Trends 2025](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-global-culture-and-trends-2025-platform-highlights-creator-innovation-and-regional-content-shifts/)
- **Platform scale:** Shorts passed 200B daily views by June 2025 — [Teleprompter blog (vendor, citing YouTube)](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/youtube-shorts-statistics). Shorts passed 9 trillion total views as of 21 Nov 2025 — [Wikipedia: YouTube Shorts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube_Shorts). India is described as the 2nd-largest Shorts market after the US — [Crelo AI guide (vendor)](https://creloai.com/blogs/youtube-shorts-india-creator-guide-2026). One estimate gives India 24% of global Shorts users vs. US 12% — [ShortsIntel (vendor estimate, treat cautiously)](https://www.shortsintel.com/statistics/youtube-shorts)
- **Monetisation (vendor estimates):** Indian creators "typically earn ₹100–400 per 100,000 Shorts views" — [Crelo AI (vendor)](https://creloai.com/blogs/youtube-shorts-india-creator-guide-2026). "$50–500 per million views" for Shorts — [Virvid blog (vendor, no cited data)](https://virvid.ai/blog/faceless-channel-monetization-timeline-2026)
- **Faceless-vs-face claim:** a blog claims craft-quality faceless channels grow "40–60% faster than face channels in the same niches." This is unverified; it is a single blog with no data shown. — [garywu.github.io faceless playbook](https://garywu.github.io/posts/faceless-channel-playbook)
- **Global hands-only precedent:** 5-Minute Crafts uses "a top-down camera [that] displays objects on a solid-color surface while only a person's hands appear in the frame." Its subscribers and views "started to grow rapidly" in 2017. It is run by TheSoul Publishing (100+ channels), so it is a studio, not a solo creator. — [Wikipedia: 5-Minute Crafts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-Minute_Crafts); [Wikipedia: Bright Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_Side_(YouTube_channel))
- **Global art benchmark (face shown, long-form origin):** Zach Hsieh went viral from a 2018 "re-drawing" video and later passed 25M subscribers — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Hsieh)

### Inferences
- Indian craft channels with very large Shorts view averages (Crafter Aditi, Artkala) are mostly **face-on, broad "DIY everything"** channels that started years ago. Their growth is slowing (0.41%/month). A new faceless entrant is unlikely to win head-on in "generic DIY/craft hacks."
- Non-verbal or low-language Shorts are officially noted as a 2025 growth driver in India. A hands-only format with short Hinglish voice-over (or captions) fits this, and can reach beyond Hindi viewers.
- Mid-tier craft channels (about 16K average Shorts views at 2M subscribers) suggest generic craft Shorts views are spread thinly. Specialised, searchable or seasonal topics probably do better per video, but I found no cross-niche dataset to prove it.

### Gaps
- No source gave average Shorts views or engagement **by sub-niche** (mehndi, rangoli, crochet, embroidery, resin, clay/miniature, painting, mandala, calligraphy, quilling/origami, upcycling, home decor, festive, school projects, jewellery, ASMR, budget hacks). vidIQ, Playboard and Social Blade category pages could not be opened.
- No 2025–2026 data on which sub-niches are rising globally (e.g., whether crochet or clay miniatures are growing on Shorts). The searches on crochet, resin and clay returned no growth data.

Mehndi and "best out of waste / school project" craft have many Indian and Pakistani channels in the 0.6M–2M+ subscriber range and very many generic uploads, which signals heavy supply. Hindi crochet, by contrast, shows mostly small tutorial channels with no visible large players. That points to a possible gap, but it is not confirmed with numbers.

- **Mehndi demand is event-driven:** 2026 trend pieces describe a "craze for 3D mehndi, golden mehndi among newlyweds ahead of Karwa Chauth" — [Loktej](https://english.loktej.com/article/34086/karwa-chauth--the-craze-for-3d-mehndi-among-married-women--beautiful-designs-will-emerge-on-hands). - **Most saturated in Hindi/India (lowest differentiation):** generic mehndi design compilations, generic "best out of waste" and school-project Shorts, and broad "5 craft hacks" formats. This is based on the lack of big Hindi players in the search results, not on measured supply or demand data.
- Mehndi has an advantage despite saturation: very high, recurring, festival-driven search intent (Karwa Chauth, Teej, Eid, weddings). A *specific* angle such as "1-minute simple mehndi for beginners/kids" or "back-hand Karwa Chauth mehndi" could still capture seasonal spikes. Hands-only is the native format for mehndi.

## Q4. Which formats work best for hands-only craft Shorts? Ideal length and hook styles

### Takeaway
The only length guidance found comes from vendor blogs, and it disagrees. For retention-optimised tutorials, 15–45 seconds is the common recommendation. One study cited secondhand found 50–60 second Shorts got far more views than very short ones. Hooks should land in the first 3 seconds; one benchmark is 70%+ of viewers staying past that point. Overhead or top-down camera framing is the standard for hands-only craft.

### Cited Findings
- 15–30 second Shorts "consistently achieve the highest retention rates (often 80%+)" — vendor blog — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention)
- An Inflow Network study of 5,400 Shorts (cited secondhand) found 50–60 second videos got about 22x more views than videos under 10 seconds. It measured views, not retention. — [Piktochart](https://piktochart.com/blog/how-long-youtube-shorts/)
- Tutorials: 20–45 seconds or 25–45 seconds suggested if pacing is tight — [Miraflow](https://miraflow.ai/blog/how-long-should-youtube-shorts-be-2026); [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/best-shorts-length-retention-2026) (vendor blogs)
- "Early retention (first few seconds) and completion rate are heavily weighted." Intro retention past 3 seconds "should ideally be above 70%." — [Miraflow best practices](https://miraflow.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-best-practices-2026-complete-guide); [AWISEE](https://awisee.com/blog/youtube-shorts-length/) (vendor blogs)
- Overhead camera angles are recommended for craft Shorts because viewers need to see hands and the work surface — [Fluxnote guide (snippet only)](https://fluxnote.io/guides/diy-crafts-youtube-shorts-ideas-2026)
- Top-down, solid-colour surface, hands-only framing is the 5-Minute Crafts template — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-Minute_Crafts)
- Non-verbal Shorts were cited by YouTube as a cross-language growth driver for Indian creators in 2025 — [Net Influencer / YouTube Culture & Trends](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-global-culture-and-trends-2025-platform-highlights-creator-innovation-and-regional-content-shifts/)

### Inferences
- Likely best-fit formats for a hands-only, low-time-budget Hinglish channel:
  1. **Finished result first, then a sped-up process** (reveal hook in 0–2 seconds, about 20–40 seconds total).
  2. **Step-by-step tutorial with short Hinglish voice-over or text** ("सिर्फ 3 steps में…"), 30–50 seconds, for searchable topics such as mehndi, crochet stitches, or school projects.
  3. **Budget/constraint hooks** ("₹50 में Diwali decor", "waste bottle से…"). This is a natural fit for India, but the evidence for it is my inference, not data.
  4. **Satisfying or ASMR process clips** with no voice. These cost the least to edit and travel across languages.
- With 3–5 hours a week, batch-filming one project from overhead and cutting it into 2–3 Shorts (reveal, tutorial, mistake/fix) is the realistic workflow. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No craft-specific A/B or retention data comparing timelapse, tutorial, POV and before/after formats.
- No data on Hinglish voice-over vs. music-only performance.

## Q5. Is search demand ("mehndi design", "school project", "rangoli design") high and recurring in India? Google Trends signals

### Takeaway
Not measured. No Google Trends data could be retrieved. Editorial and festival evidence strongly suggests mehndi and rangoli demand is **recurring and seasonal**, peaking around Karwa Chauth, Diwali, Teej, Eid and the wedding season (rangoli peaks at Diwali). School-project demand plausibly follows the academic calendar. These patterns should be checked directly on trends.google.com (region: India).

### Cited Findings
- In 2026, Karwa Chauth fell on 29 Oct and Diwali on 8 Nov, so the two festivals sit close together — [Wikipedia: Karva Chauth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karva_Chauth) / search summary
- Many publishers produce annual "Karwa Chauth / Diwali mehndi designs 2026" pages, which indicates recurring SEO demand — [ShaadiDukaan](https://www.shaadidukaan.com/blog/latest-karwa-chauth-mehndi-designs.html); [Destination Weddings India](https://destinationweddingsindia.com/mehndi-design-ideas/); [Sahu4You (mehndi + rangoli designs 2026)](https://www.sahu4you.com/mehndi-design-rangoli-design); [Anvi Couture (designs by occasion 2026)](https://anvicouture.com/blogs/news/mehndi-design-trending-mehndi-designs-as-per-occasion)
- Google Year in Search 2025 India: festival-linked food searches were prominent (Modak, Thekua, Ugadi Pachadi). The "Gemini Saree" AI trend featured among trending trends. Mehndi and rangoli did not appear in any retrieved snippet. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/year-in-google-search-2025-operation-sindoor-waqf-bill-dharmendra-kantara-ipl-vaibhav-suryavamshi-gemini-ai-dominate-3819481); [Google Year in Search 2025 India](https://trends.withgoogle.com/year-in-search/2025/in/)

### Inferences
- Mehndi has the strongest *recurring* intent among the candidates (multiple festivals plus weddings year-round). Rangoli is highly concentrated around Diwali and Pongal/Onam. School/best-out-of-waste content probably clusters around school competitions and term starts.
- A combined "festive hands-on craft" calendar (Raksha Bandhan rakhi → Navratri/Dussehra → Karwa Chauth mehndi → Diwali diya/rangoli/decor → Christmas/New Year gifts → Holi → summer-vacation kids' crafts) could keep year-round demand under one faceless brand. This is an inference.
