# Craft/DIY Shorts Channel (India, Faceless, Hindi/Hinglish): Income Beyond Shorts Ad Revenue, 2025–2026

## 1. Affiliate income (Amazon.in Associates, Flipkart, Meesho, YouTube Shopping India)

### Takeaway
Affiliate is the earliest non-ad income a craft Shorts channel can get, but per-sale commissions on craft supplies are small (roughly 3–9% on Amazon.in by most third-party tables, and official rates were not verified). YouTube Shopping in India still lists only fashion and general e-commerce merchants (Flipkart, Myntra), not Amazon. Shorts also block clickable description links, so in-video product tags or Instagram/bio links do most of the work. The effort is low (about 15–30 min/week to add links and tags), and the money is modest until views are large.

### Cited Findings
**Amazon.in Associates**
- The official Amazon.in "Associates Program Advertising Fee Schedule" snippet shows fixed rates of 6% for Kitchen, Furniture, Outdoors and **DIY & Tools**, 2% for Grocery/Amazon Fresh and 7% for Echo/Fire TV devices. The snippet was partial and undated. — [Amazon.in Associates fee schedule](https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/welcome/compensation/ref=amb_link_222615007_2)
- A user-uploaded Scribd copy titled "Amazon Associates Fee Schedule 2025" (India) lists bill payments/recharges at 30% (capped at ₹3), **"all other categories" at 5%**, and iPhones at 0%. This is a secondary source to verify. — [Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/833748325/Amazon-Associates-Commision)
- A 2026 India guide lists Luxury Beauty, Beauty, Grooming and Amazon Fashion at 10%, which conflicts with the 5% "all other" figure. — [WsCubeTech 2026 guide](https://www.wscubetech.com/blog/?p=15336)
- Third-party India tables conflict widely. Cuelinks (a network, Nov 2026) shows effective payout of about 3.74% for Books and Toys & Games, about 1.52% for Home, and an overall range of 0.32%–19.01% via its network. FluxNote shows Books at 7%, Toys at 4–5% and Home & Kitchen at 4–5%. Earnfromhomeindia shows Home/Kitchen/Garden at 9%, Books at 8% and Toys at 7%. — [Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-guide/); [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/amazon-india-affiliate-commission-rates-2026); [Earnfromhomeindia](https://earnfromhomeindia.com/en/10-amazon-affiliate-program-india-setup-guide-2026/)

**YouTube Shopping affiliate (India)**
- It launched in India in Oct 2024 with **Flipkart and Myntra** as partners. — [afaqs](https://afaqs.com/news/digital/youtube-shopping-expands-in-india-teams-up-with-flipkart-and-myntra-7354069); [Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/business/companies/youtube-launches-shopping-affiliate-programme-in-india-teams-up-with-flipkart-myntra-3248631)

Each retailer sets its own commission and attribution window, and the rate shows up while tagging. Up to 30 products can be tagged per video. Creators initially receive 100% of the commission. Payouts go through AdSense after 60–120 days to clear returns. — [YourStory, Oct 2024](https://yourstory.com/2024/10/youtube-expands-shopping-programme-india); [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/indian-youtube-creators-can-earn-by-tagging-flipkart-and-myntra-products-in-videos-know-how-2024-10-25-958835)

These conflict and remain unresolved. — [Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-arrives-in-india/); [T2 Online](https://t2online.in/tech/tech-news/youtube-expands-shopping-experience-programme-in-india-with-nykaa-and-purplle/2001531)
- Social Media Today reports that the Shopping affiliate threshold was lowered (from 1,000 to 500 subscribers) and lists India among eligible countries. I could not open the article to confirm its date or India specifics. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-expands-shopping-affiliate-program-access/815754/)
- Product tags work in Shorts, but Shorts block clickable links in descriptions and pinned comments. — [Logie](https://logie.ai/news/youtube-affiliate-shopping-2025/); [Virvid 2026 guide](https://virvid.ai/blog/youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-shorts-guide-2026)
- No source found lists Amazon as a YouTube Shopping merchant in India. — (search finding, multiple sources above)

**Flipkart / Meesho / Shopsy direct affiliate**
- Flipkart's rates are variable, "up to 12%", with electronics at about 1–2% (affiliate blog). Cuelinks says Flipkart's own influencer program is not openly accepting social-only creators. Shopsy offers up to 10.8% via Cuelinks. — [MonetizeDeal](https://blog.monetizedeal.com/flipkart-affiliate-program); [Cuelinks: Flipkart influencer program](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/flipkart-influencer-program-eligibility-commission-best-alternative-for-small-influencers/)
- The Meesho affiliate program offers "up to 15%" (aggregator claims). Registration is free, but approval can be selective. — [Cuelinks: Meesho affiliate](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/meesho-affiliate-program-a-complete-guide-from-registering-to-earning-commissions-online/)
- Flipkart and Meta launched an affiliate programme using Facebook product tags. This route matters if she cross-posts to Facebook/Instagram. — [Newskart](https://www.newskart.com/flipkart-and-meta-launch-affiliate-programme-how-facebook-product-tags-can-help-creators-earn/)

### Inferences
- Example math (assumed figures): resin kits at ₹800–1,500 with a 5% commission earn about ₹40–75 per sale. 50 sales/month would bring only about ₹2,000–3,750. Affiliate is a supplement, not a main income at small scale.
- The best craft products to link are likely items viewers can't easily find locally and that appear in the video: resin starter kits, silicone moulds, crochet hook sets and yarn bundles, acrylic/fabric paint sets, clay kits, and glue guns. Mehndi cones are cheap (low ₹ per sale) and are often bought locally, so they probably make weak affiliate items. No source gave sales data by craft product, so this is unverified.
- Aggregator networks (Cuelinks, EarnKaro) are the practical entry point for a small creator, because they let social-only creators earn on Flipkart/Meesho/Amazon without direct program approval.
- Time to availability: Amazon Associates can be joined from day one. It needs 3 qualifying sales within 180 days to stay active, a standard Associates rule that I did not re-verify for India in 2025. YouTube Shopping requires YPP plus the subscriber threshold.

### Gaps
- I could not open the official, dated Amazon.in fee schedule. It is unknown whether "Arts & Crafts", "Toys" or "Home" are 5%, 6% or another figure in 2025–26. Whitelist affiliate-program.amazon.in to verify.
- The current India YouTube Shopping eligibility threshold (10K vs 500/1,000 subscribers) and merchant list could not be confirmed (support.google.com blocked).
- No data was found on which craft products convert best through affiliate links in India.

## 2. Selling her own handmade products or DIY kits

### Takeaway
Selling her own products is the highest-margin route and the one Indian craft creators most often turn into real income, especially through Instagram/WhatsApp DMs. It is also the most time-hungry route (making, packing, shipping, customer chat), so with 3–5 hrs/week it only works as made-to-order, small-batch work or as kits. Meesho charges 0% commission but suits low-price items. Amazon Karigar caps referral fees at 8% or less. Etsy India charges about 6.5% plus payment fees and mostly serves export buyers.

### Cited Findings
- Meesho charges **zero commission** on almost all categories, but shipping, return-related penalties and other charges still apply. Its high competition and returns make profits hard for small sellers. A low-authority blog contradicts this with a 10–15% claim. — [Inc42 on Meesho model](https://inc42.com/features/decoding-meesho-business-model-seller-centric-revenue-engine/); [TheGSTCo 2025 guide](https://thegstco.com/blogs/ecommerce/meesho-seller-guide-how-to-sell-on-meesho-in-2025); [upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/meeshos-bharat-playbook-how-zero-commission-and-a-%e2%82%b9300-price-point-built-indias-third-largest-e-commerce-platform/)
- Meesho's typical price point is about ₹300. — [upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/meeshos-bharat-playbook-how-zero-commission-and-a-%e2%82%b9300-price-point-built-indias-third-largest-e-commerce-platform/)
- Shopsy (Flipkart) is described as focusing on local and handmade items at low prices. — [BlendNow](https://www.blendnow.com/blog/meesho-vs-shopsy)
- **Amazon Karigar**: the referral fee for artisans is "capped at 8% or less, depending on category" (date unclear). Past full fee waivers ran for 10 weeks in 2020 and 2 weeks in Aug–Sep 2021, and no 2025 waiver was found. — [Amazon seller blog](https://sell.amazon.in/seller-blog/amazon-karigar-what-it-holds-for-you-as-an-artisan); [Amazon press 2020](https://press.aboutamazon.com/in/2020/9/amazons-stand-for-handmade-initiative-helps-karigar-saheli-sellers-double-their-sale); [Amazon press 2021](https://press.aboutamazon.com/in/2021/8/amazon-india-launches-karigar-mela-in-partnership-with-tribes-india)
- **Etsy (India sellers)**: $0.20 per listing plus a 6.5% transaction fee on item price and shipping. India payment processing is reported as 5% + ₹25 per order (unverified, sources conflict). A one-time setup fee of about $10–29 and a regulatory operating fee for India are also reported. — [Etsy fee basics](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360035902374-Beginner-s-Guide-to-Etsy-Fees); [iThink Logistics](https://www.ithinklogistics.com/blog/does-etsy-work-in-india/); [ShipGlobal](https://shipglobal.in/blogs/etsy-new-sellers/)
- **Indian crochet creator-sellers (examples)**:
  - A Gujarat couple runs a crochet bag business from home at about **₹2.5 lakh/month**.   - Floreal (Ludhiana, 20-year-old founder) began with 30–40 Instagram DM orders. It now handles about 700 orders/day (about 500 via Blinkit, 200 via its website). The headline revenue figure was garbled in the snippet. — [Startuppedia](https://startuppedia.in/young-entrepreneur/this-20-yo-introverted-ludhiana-girl-built-a-528-crore-biz-in-2-years-through-handicraft-crochet-flower-sale-now-empowering-300-women-11149660)
  - Kanchan Bhadani (LoopHoop, Jharkhand) makes crochet toys with revenue of more than ₹14 lakh/year. — [Mad4India](https://mad4india.com/?p=29492)
  - Croise (Kolkata) earned ₹5 lakh revenue since Oct 2025 from a ₹1 lakh investment. — [Startuppedia](https://startuppedia.in/smbs/kolkata-woman-quit-job-to-pursue-her-crochet-hobby-invested-1-lakh-savings-clocked-rs-5-lakh-revenue-in-3-months-10992249)

### Inferences
- With 3–5 hrs/week, the most workable options are (a) made-to-order, high-value personalised pieces (resin keepsakes, name plates, crochet gifts) taken through WhatsApp/Instagram DMs, or (b) pre-assembled DIY kits matching her viewed Shorts, with fulfilment outsourced or done in batches. Meesho's about ₹300 price point and return-heavy dynamics suit low-effort mass products, not her time-limited handmade work.
- Festive spikes (Raksha Bandhan, Diwali, Karwa Chauth for mehndi) are where handmade sellers make outsized revenue, as the Gujarat example shows.
- The named examples report revenue, not profit, and most are full-time operations, so they show what's possible, not what to expect with 3–5 hrs/week.
- This route is available from day one with no subscriber threshold, but sales depend on audience trust.

### Gaps
- No reliable data was found on typical margins (%) for handmade resin, crochet or clay products in India. Margin claims would be unsourced.
- Faceless, Shorts-first craft creators selling products were not found specifically. The examples are mostly Instagram-led sellers.
- Current Meesho/Shopsy shipping and return-fee rates for small sellers were not verified.

## 3. Paid workshops, online classes and digital products (PDF designs, patterns)

### Takeaway
Online craft workshops in India cluster at ₹299–₹1,500 for mass-market recorded or live sessions, and ₹3,500–₹4,000 for multi-session courses. Digital products (mehndi or rangoli design PDFs, crochet patterns) are low-ticket (international pattern benchmarks are about £2.50–£8) but need near-zero ongoing effort once made. Both suit a time-limited creator better than physical products. Instamojo, Superprofile and Graphy-type tools handle delivery.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Example pricing for an Indian Hindi audience (estimate): mehndi/rangoli design PDF packs at ₹49–₹199, crochet pattern PDFs at ₹99–₹299, a recorded beginner mini-course at ₹299–₹999, and a live Zoom workshop at ₹499–₹1,500 per seat. These fit the ₹299–₹1,500 band seen above.
- A recorded course or PDF pack is a one-time effort of about 10–20 hrs that can sell indefinitely, which suits 3–5 hrs/week. Live workshops need a fixed weekly slot and some on-camera or hands-on live presence. A faceless creator can still run them with an overhead hands-cam.
- Faceless channels can sell workshops, but trust and conversion are usually lower without a face or voice. Hindi voiceover helps. This is an inference, not sourced.
- This route can start early (no subscriber threshold), but realistically needs a few thousand engaged followers and a WhatsApp/Telegram list to convert.

### Gaps
- No INR prices were found for Indian crochet classes or for mehndi/rangoli PDFs sold by Indian creators.
- No case study gave workshop or digital-product revenue for an Indian craft YouTuber.
- Graphy pricing/fees for creators were not researched (time limit).

## 4. Brand deals for craft creators in India

### Takeaway
Art and craft brands (Pidilite/Fevicol/Fevicreate, Faber-Castell, Camlin, DOMS, Brustro) do run creator and influencer campaigns, often using DIY or parenting creators in bulk. No published rates are specific to craft creators. General Indian micro-creator rates are about ₹500–₹5,000 per reel below 10K followers, and about ₹5,000–₹80,000 per reel or short at 10K–100K. Small art creators often start with barter (free products).

### Cited Findings
- Kofluence (via search summary) gives about ₹500–5,000 per reel for creators under 10K followers. Other guides give ₹5,000–25,000, ₹5,000–75,000 or ₹8,000–80,000 per reel for 10K–100K micro creators. — [Kofluence 2025 report](https://www.kofluence.com/?p=63320); [Boss Wallah 2026](https://bosswallah.com/blog/brand-collaborations/micro-influencer-rates-in-india-what-to-actually-charge-in-2026/); [upgrowth 2026](https://upgrowth.in/influencer-marketing-pricing-india-2026/); [TickTime rate card 2026](https://ticktime.media/blogs/influencer-rate-card-india-2026-instagram-reel-story-youtube-video-pricing)

- India's creator economy is about ₹3,500 crore, driven by e-commerce and FMCG brands. — [Storyboard18 / Kofluence](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/creator-economy-in-india-hits-rs-3500-crore-fueled-by-ecomm-fmcg-sectors-kofluence-73457.htm)

### Inferences
- Brand deals usually become realistic around 10K+ subscribers or followers with steady views. Below that, expect mostly barter or product seeding. A faceless hands-only format is a good fit for product-demo integrations (glue, paints, markers, resin), which these brands' campaigns already favour.
- Likely sponsors beyond the big names include D2C craft-supply startups (resin, clay and yarn sellers on Amazon or Instagram) and quick-commerce-listed art brands. These are smaller budgets but more open to micro creators.
- Effort per deal is moderate: about 1–3 hrs for briefing, a custom Short and revisions. One deal per month fits the time budget.
- Influencer platforms and agencies (Kofluence, Qoruz, Collabstr) are the entry channels.

### Gaps
- No rate card was specific to craft/DIY creators in India, and no YouTube-Shorts-specific INR rates were found. Rates above are general micro-influencer figures from agency or vendor blogs.
- Whether Brustro, DOMS or Camlin pay small creators or only barter was not found.

## 5. Craft/DIY niche Shorts RPM (India)

### Takeaway
No reliable craft-specific Shorts RPM data for India exists in public sources. The only DIY-specific figure is a vendor-blog estimate of ₹5–15 RPM for DIY & Crafts in India, roughly in line with general India Shorts estimates of ₹5–40.

### Cited Findings
- DIY & Crafts YouTube ad revenue in India is estimated at ₹5–15 RPM (vendor blog estimate). — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-to-monetize-diy-crafts-channel-india)
- India Shorts RPM is estimated at ₹5–40 per 1,000 views, versus ₹15–300+ for long-form. Shorts RPM in India is estimated at about 10% of long-form. — [Boss Wallah](https://bosswallah.com/blog/creator-hub/1000-views-on-youtube-india-income-rpm-cpm-breakdown/); [WsCubeTech](https://www.wscubetech.com/blog/youtube-income-per-1000-views/)
- Hindi or regional content and a mostly Indian audience earn lower CPM than English content. — [WsCubeTech](https://www.wscubetech.com/blog/youtube-income-per-1000-views/)
- Art channel RPM in India is estimated at ₹8–20 (vendor estimate). — [FluxNote art](https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-to-monetize-art-channel-india)

### Inferences
- At about ₹5–15 per 1,000 Shorts views, 1M monthly views brings only about ₹5,000–15,000. This explains why sources estimate brand deals or sponsorships at 40–50% of a mid-size Indian channel's income ([FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-much-youtubers-earn-india-2026), vendor estimate). Products, kits and courses typically outweigh ads for craft creators.

### Gaps
- No creator-reported Studio screenshots or case studies for Hindi craft Shorts RPM were found.
